# Emergency Supply Chain Game
## Tutorial

**[Yiseon Choi] | [choi.1908@@osu.edu]**

---

# Today’s challenge

You are part of a team managing emergency supplies after a flood.

Your job is to decide **how many bottles of water to order each day**.

> Aim to keep supplies available without building unnecessary inventory.

---

# What you will do

1. Join your assigned team and select your assigned role.
2. Make one order decision when it is your turn.
3. Watch the supply chain update after all roles submit.
4. Repeat for each game day.
5. Review your team's final results.

**Your decisions affect both your own costs and your team's outcome.**

---

Open the game at:

# Join the correct team

1. Select **Play Game**.
2. Choose your assigned **Team Number**.
3. Choose your assigned **Team Letter**.
4. Select **your assigned role**.
5. Answer the brief Quick Question, then select **Continue**.

**Important:** Everyone on one team uses the same number and letter. Each teammate must choose a different role.

---

# The supply chain

Supplies move downstream toward the people who need them:

## Federal Stockpile -> Regional Hubs -> State/Local Hubs -> End Users

| Role | Your place in the chain |
|---|---|
| Federal Stockpile | Source of emergency supplies |
| Regional Hubs | Coordinate supply across regions |
| State/Local Hubs | Move supplies to local areas |
| End Users | Receive supplies where demand occurs |

---

# What the game tracks

For your role, the game shows the **previous day's** supply-chain status.

| Measure | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Inventory | Units currently available at your tier |
| Backorders | Unfilled demand or requests waiting to be met |
| Incoming shipments | Units already on their way to you |
| Order history | The quantities you chose in earlier days |
| Budget | Your starting endowment minus accumulated cost |

Use these signals to make your next order decision.

---

# Your objective: control costs

Each day, costs accumulate from three sources:

| Cost | What causes it |
|---|---|
| Inventory cost | Holding units that remain in inventory |
| Backlog cost | Having unfilled demand or requests |
| Team penalty | End Users have any backlog, affecting the whole team |

**Lower total cost is better.** The final results screen ranks players by total cost.

---

# Delivery delay and uncertainty

Orders do not arrive immediately. A lead time separates an order from its arrival.

That means an order today should consider:

- what is already in inventory;
- what is already in transit;
- outstanding backorders;
- demand from the prior day; and
- the possibility of a disruption or demand increase.

Do not expect one large order to solve every problem instantly.

---

# When it is your turn

The team takes turns in this fixed order:

## End Users -> State/Local Hubs -> Regional Hubs -> Federal Stockpile

When the Team Status panel indicates **YOUR TURN**:

1. Review the available status information.
2. Enter an order quantity from **0 to 1,000**.
3. Select **SUBMIT**.

Your entry is final for that day.

---

# When it is not your turn

Your order field is locked until your role becomes active.

- Check the Team Status panel to see who is currently acting.
- After submitting, you will see that your order is recorded.
- Wait for the remaining roles to submit.
- The next game day begins only after all four roles have submitted.

If chat is available in your condition, use it only as directed by the facilitator.

---

# Information may differ by condition

Some teams can see the status of every tier. Others can see only their own tier.

Some teams may also have access to team chat.

These differences are intentional parts of the study. Please make decisions using **only the information available in your game** and follow the communication instructions you receive.

---

# Responding to disruptions

The game may show a disaster warning on particular days.

When you see a warning:

1. Recheck inventory, backorders, and shipments in transit.
2. Account for the delivery delay before expecting new supplies.
3. Balance shortage risk against the cost of excess inventory.
4. Submit the quantity you judge best with the information available.

There is no single announced “correct” order. The exercise is about decision-making under uncertainty.

# At the end of the game

The game displays final results for the team:

- rank;
- role;
- inventory cost;
- backlog cost; and
- total cost.

When you reach the end screen, **do not restart or close the page**. Wait for the facilitator's next instruction.
